Solution Architecture

Good architecture ensures that systems fit together, grow with your business and remain maintainable five years from now. I design solutions that match your requirements, your budget and your team – vendor-neutral and clearly documented.

Typical questions

  • Which systems do we really need – and how do they fit together?
  • Off-the-shelf software, cloud service or custom development?
  • How do we connect a new system to ERP, CRM or existing databases?
  • How do we avoid isolated solutions and vendor lock-in?

What I do

  • Assessing your system landscape and data flows
  • Designing the target architecture including interfaces and integration patterns
  • Technology and product selection based on transparent criteria
  • Evaluating risks, operating effort and total cost of ownership
  • Architecture documentation that your team and vendors can work with

Your result

  • A clear target architecture with an implementation roadmap
  • Decision papers for management
  • Fewer bad investments thanks to early evaluation of alternatives
  • If required, support with or delivery of the implementation

Approach

Getting to know each other, analysis, concept, implementation – you decide after each phase how to proceed.
