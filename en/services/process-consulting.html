Process Consulting

Before introducing new software, you need a clear picture of how work actually flows through your organisation. I analyse your processes, uncover friction and work with you to define lean target processes – the foundation for any meaningful digitalisation.

Typical starting points

  • Information is entered several times, forwarded by email or kept in spreadsheets
  • Workflows depend on individual people and are not documented anywhere
  • New software is to be introduced, but the requirements are unclear
  • Departments work with different systems that do not talk to each other

What I do

  • Capturing current processes in workshops and interviews with your departments
  • Visualising workflows, e.g. as BPMN or swimlane diagrams
  • Identifying manual hand-offs, duplicate work and bottlenecks
  • Defining target processes and business requirements
  • Assessing which steps can be automated or digitalised – and which cannot

Your result

  • Transparent, documented workflows that new employees can follow
  • A prioritised list of improvements with effort and benefit
  • Solid requirements for software selection or custom development
  • If required, technical implementation from a single source

Approach

Getting to know each other, analysis, concept, implementation – you decide after each phase how to proceed.
