Infrastructure – Consulting & Implementation

A stable infrastructure is the foundation for everything else. I help you choose the right platform – cloud, on-premises or hybrid – and then build it myself: secure, automated and documented so your team can run it.

Typical starting points

  • Servers and applications have grown over the years and are hard to maintain
  • A move to the cloud is planned, but cost and effort are unclear
  • Applications should run in containers, but the know-how is missing in-house
  • Outages or security gaps are only noticed when it is too late

What I do

  • Analysing your existing infrastructure and defining a target platform
  • Building environments in Microsoft Azure or on-premises
  • Containerising applications and running them on Kubernetes
  • Migrating applications and databases with minimal downtime
  • Infrastructure as code, backups, monitoring and alerting

Your result

  • Infrastructure that runs reliably at predictable cost
  • Automated, reproducible environments instead of manual work
  • Visibility into the health and utilisation of your systems
  • Documentation and handover, so you are not dependent on me

Approach

Getting to know each other, analysis, concept, implementation – you decide after each phase how to proceed.
